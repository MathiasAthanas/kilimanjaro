RESULT
The navigation and page-flow implementation for Phases 7 and 8 is in place.
Phase 9 checks pass for route parity, typecheck, tests and build. The repository
wide lint gate is not green (718 errors, 31 warnings); a live authenticated
walkthrough of all roles was not available. Treat this as implementation-ready
for a human role walkthrough, not as a fully cleared production release.

PHASE 7 — FINANCE AND OPERATIONS
- FINANCE navigation now starts at invoice, payment and receipt ledgers. Existing
  generate, cash, bank, pending-approval, fee setup, spending, stores, assets,
  reporting, export and audit routes remain discoverable. Ledger header actions
  link to existing posting/setup pages; detail and form headers return to their
  directories. No posting, approval, refund, void or export handlers changed.
- PRINCIPAL retains its separate finance oversight and approval navigation.
  HOD fund requests and manager finance/fund approval paths remain in place.
- ADMIN timetable hub/sheets/new sheet/venues/activity types, notification
  templates/send/logs, announcements, reports and system audit remain grouped
  through existing sidebar and contextual tasks. Notification template/log
  screens now state loading, error and empty conditions; manual-send wording
  no longer implies a scheduling feature and has a deterministic cancel path.

PHASE 8 — GROUP AND SCHOOL SCOPE
- Group school lists and school switching honor session.scope and assigned
  schoolIds. ADMIN with SCHOOL scope is not treated as a group user. Group-only
  routes clear selected-school context; changing schools inside a school route
  keeps the current section. Group roles see school-specific work links after
  selecting a school and retain Back to Group.
- Head of Finance selection from /finance-group now opens that school's
  invoice ledger, rather than the principal portal. The group finance table
  exposes an authorized school-finance next step. Super Admin retains the
  school administration table, including schools absent from the scorecards.
- Cross-stage promotion, performance engine, stores and assets remain reachable
  in manager navigation. Shared notifications, announcements, search, profile,
  help, exports and audit entry points remain present for their prior roles.
