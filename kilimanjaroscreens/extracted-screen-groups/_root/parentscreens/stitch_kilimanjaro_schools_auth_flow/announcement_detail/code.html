End of Term Exams Begin Monday 28 April

Principal Dr. M. Kijazi

schedule 2 hours ago

Dear Parents and Guardians, as we approach the conclusion of Term 1, we wish to formally notify you that the **End of Term Examinations** for all grades will commence on **Monday, 28 April 2026**.

Attendance during this period is mandatory. We kindly request that all students arrive at the school premises no later than 7:45 AM to ensure a prompt start to the morning sessions. Students must be equipped with the required stationery and should be in full school uniform.

Key Requirements

  • Valid Student Identification Card
  • Scientific calculators (for secondary students)
  • Transparent pencil cases only

Preparation is key to peak performance. We encourage parents to support their children by providing a quiet study environment and ensuring adequate rest during the exam week.
