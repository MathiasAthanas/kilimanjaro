# Go-live checklist for staff: in campus / off campus, hostel and billing

Sign in at https://manage.kilimanjaroschools.site. Do the steps **in this order**. Each person only does their own part; the arrows show who is waiting for whom.

Pick the school first (the school switcher at the top) before doing anything below. Everything here is set for one school at a time.

---

## 1. Finance officer (or Head of Finance) — before any invoices are generated

Menu: Finance, Fee Setup.

1. **Payment Info**: press *Add account* and enter each bank account and mobile-money number parents should pay into (bank or network, account name, number, a short description such as "Fees"). Only active accounts are printed on invoices.
2. **Invoice Look**:
   - Invoices carry the Kilimanjaro Schools logo automatically. Upload a different **logo** or a **stamp** only if the school wants one (PNG or JPG).
   - Pick the accent colour and write the note at the bottom.
   - Choose the **residence rule**: when a student moves between in campus and off campus in the middle of a term, should the new fee apply *from next term*, *from this term*, or *ask each time*.
   - Press **Save changes**, then **Preview invoice**. Check the logo, colours and accounts on the sample.
3. **Fee Structures / Fee Matrix**: check that prices are set per class level (Class 1, Form 2). If a boarder pays more, add a second price for the same level marked *Boarding*; the normal price stays for day scholars.
4. **Payment Plans**: press *New plan*. Choose which fees are split (usually tuition), then add the instalments (a percentage, a fixed amount or "the rest", due on a date or a number of days after the invoice). The right-hand panel shows what a parent would see. Percentages must add up to 100%.
5. ⛔ **Wait** until the admission office has classified the students (step 3) **before** pressing *Generate Invoices*. Students who are not classified only receive prices that are not tied to boarding or day.

## 2. Administrator

1. **Users**, *Create user*: create the **Matron**, **Patron** and **Admissions Officer** accounts. Each person is given the school they work in.
2. **Boarding**, *Add dormitory*: type the name and number of beds, tick the matron or patron in charge, and press *Create dormitory*. The beds are created for you. Repeat for each dormitory.

## 3. Admissions officer (or administrator)

Menu: In / Off Campus.

1. Look at the tiles at the top. *Not classified* is the number of students still to do.
2. In the **By class** table, press **In campus** or **Off campus** on a class row to classify everyone in that class who is not classified yet. For a mixed class, open the list instead (*Not classified* tab), tick the boarders and press *Mark in campus*, then do the rest as off campus.
3. Students who already have an answer are never changed by the bulk buttons.
4. Repeat until *Not classified* shows 0.
5. From now on, every new student **must** be marked in campus or off campus when they are admitted (New Inquiry, converting an applicant, or Enrol Student). The system will not let you finish without it.

> Finance can now generate invoices.

## 4. Finance officer — generating invoices

1. Finance, *Generate Invoices*: choose the year, term and classes, and generate.
2. Open one invoice of a boarder and one of a day scholar. Check the prices, the instalments under *Payment plan*, and download the PDF.
3. Invoices that already existed before today have no plan. Use *Payment Plans*, *Invoices already sent*: choose the term and press *Apply plans*.
4. For a family with a special arrangement, open the invoice, *Edit dates*, change the amounts and dates (they must add up to the invoice total) and give a reason.
5. *Notify parent* on an invoice sends the guardian a message about it. Parents can also download the PDF themselves.

## 5. Matron or patron — every day

Menu: Today.

1. The cards at the top are your tasks: **Waiting for a bed**, **Roll call**, **Leave requests**, **Returning today**. A number means something needs you.
2. **Waiting for a bed**: choose the dormitory in *Place into*, tick the students (or press *Place* on one) and press *Place selected*. Each student gets the next free bed.
3. **Roll call**: everyone starts as present. Only tap *Absent*, *On leave* or *Excused* for the exceptions, then *Save roll call*. You can correct it again the same day.
4. **Leave and returns**: approve or decline requests, press *Student has left* when they go, and *Student is back* when they return. Use *Record a leave* for a new request.
5. Wrong placement? On Today, open the dormitory, press *Remove* beside the student; they go back to the waiting list.

## 6. Changing where a student stays later

Administrator, admissions officer or principal: open the student, *Where the student stays*, press **Change**.

- Pick the new choice and when it takes effect (now, or from next term).
- Becoming off campus frees the dormitory bed automatically. If the student is away on leave at that moment, record their return first.
- Becoming in campus puts the student on the matron's waiting list.
- Finance then sees the invoice marked for review. They preview the fee difference, confirm with a reason, and payments already made are never lost.

---

## Quick checks after the first day

- [ ] Payment Info shows the real accounts and *Preview invoice* looks right.
- [ ] *Not classified* is 0 on the In / Off Campus page.
- [ ] A boarder's invoice shows the boarding price; a day scholar's shows the day price.
- [ ] Each matron sees only her own dormitories and the boarders waiting.
- [ ] One invoice PDF downloaded and checked by the bursar.

## If something looks wrong

Do not repeat the action. Tell the system administrator what you pressed and what you saw (a screenshot helps). Nothing in this release deletes records: changes are recorded in the history of each student and in the finance audit log, so they can be explained and corrected.
